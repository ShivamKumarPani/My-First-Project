About the School

A Government School Rooted in Kharsawan Since 1948

+2 GOVT. HS KHARSAWAN was established in 1948 and is managed by the Department of Education. It is located in a rural area, in the Kharsawan block of Saraikela Kharsawan district, Jharkhand.

The school offers Grades 9 to 12 and is co-educational, without an attached pre-primary section. Hindi is the medium of instruction, and the school is approachable by an all-weather road. The academic session begins in April.

The school does not operate as a shift-school and currently does not provide a mid-day meal (Not Applicable).
